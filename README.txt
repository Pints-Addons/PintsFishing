Pint's Fishing v1.0 - fish with one key (WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsFishing
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

HOW TO FISH
  1. Options > Keybindings > Pint's Fishing > "Cast and hook": pick a key.
  2. Press it to cast.
  3. When the bobber splashes, press it again - it hooks the fish (it interacts with the bobber through the game's
     soft targeting, which Pint's Fishing turns on while you fish).
  4. Repeat. The loot is taken at once if auto loot is on.

ON YOUR ACTION BARS (use the mouse buttons you already have bound)
  /pfish macro (or the "Get the fishing macro" button in /pfish) makes a "PintsFishing" macro and puts it on your
  cursor: drop it on any action bar button. That button's key - a mouse button too - then casts, and while the line
  is out it hooks the fish. Clicking the button casts as well.

FISH TRACKER
  While you fish, a small panel shows where you are, your casts, the fish you've caught, each kind of catch with how
  many and its share, your Fishing skill (with the levels gained this session) and how long you've been fishing.
  Click its title for a drop-down of views: here or all waters, for this session, this character's all time, or
  every character's. Drag to move.
  It hides a minute after you stop. /pfish tracker shows or hides it; /pfish reset starts the session over.

WHERE TO FISH   (/pfish where, /pfish where gold, or the button on the tracker)
  For skill: the zones where your skill catches every time, and the ones where only a few get away, nearest first.
  For gold: zones ranked by what an hour there is worth - what bites there, priced from Pint's Auctions (else
  vendor), your catch chance and your own time per cast. Hover a zone for what bites there. Zone skills are
  Classic's; WoW Forever's new waters aren't listed. Your own catches (20+) at a zone replace the built-in list.

COOK   (/pfish cook, or the button on the tracker)
  Every fish in your bags that cooks into something, what it makes, and what it's worth raw or cooked, at the
  auction house or a vendor - with the best way picked out. Cook buttons cook the lot (your Cooking window has to be
  open); Campfire lights a Basic Campfire; Open Cooking opens it.

ALSO
  - Double right-click to cast (turn it on in /pfish): with a fishing pole in hand, two quick right-clicks on the
    world cast.
  - Clearer splashes: while you fish, music and ambience go quiet (your sound-effects volume stays as it is).
    Everything goes back 20 seconds after you stop.
  - Fast loot: with auto loot on, everything in any loot window is taken at once and the window doesn't flash up
    (it shows if something can't be taken - bags full, a roll).
  /pfish    options

NOTE (Classic Era)
  The game's soft targeting reaches less far than a long cast: if the bobber lands too far away the key can't reach
  it - click the bobber instead, or cast a little closer.
